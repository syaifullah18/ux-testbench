Modular Research Toolkit

Five research methods in one engine.

Combine multiple methods in a single participant journey, or run targeted standalone studies.

Survey & Screener

Screener questions, Likert scales, multi-choice, and open-ended feedback with conditional skip logic.

When to use: Participant qualification, satisfaction ratings (CSAT/NPS), and qualitative discovery.

A/B Prototype Testing

Embed real HTML or Figma prototypes side-by-side. Track time-on-task, clickpaths, success rates, and single-ease questions.

When to use: Validating design revisions before engineering handoff with rigorous metrics.

Card Sorting

Open, closed, or hybrid card sorts with responsive drag-and-drop. Generates similarity matrices and hierarchical cluster dendrograms.

When to use: Structuring navigation menus, content taxonomies, and feature categorization.

Tree Testing

Evaluate text-only information hierarchies. Measure directness, success rate, and path backtracking without visual distraction.

When to use: Validating menu structures and finding exactly where participants get lost.

First-Click Testing

Upload static mockups and measure where users click first. First-click success correlates directly with end-to-end task completion.

When to use: Checking visual hierarchy, banner efficacy, and initial navigation instinct.

Multi-Module Journeys

Chain screeners, card sorts, interactive prototype tasks, and exit interviews into one participant session.

Flexible prerequisite rules and audience targeting included.

Ethics & Architecture

You own your research data. Completely.

Unlike commercial testing platforms that train proprietary models or resell participant panels, UX Testbench is built for complete privacy and isolation.

SQLite-per-project: Each study has an isolated database file.
No Third-Party Trackers: Zero ad scripts, zero tracking pixels.
MIT Licensed: Self-host on your own infrastructure forever.
