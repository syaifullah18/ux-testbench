{#- ---------------------------------------------------------------- statistics -#}

Statistical honesty

A tool that will tell you it does not know.

Most testing tools run a t-test on eight participants and print a winner. Task times are not normally distributed and eight people are not a sample, so that number is theatre.

UX Testbench uses an exact sign test, bootstrapped intervals, and a verdict with three states instead of two. Every verdict shows its p-value and how many paired participants it rests on.
