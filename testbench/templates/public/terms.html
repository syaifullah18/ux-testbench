Terms of Use

Standards for ethical, secure, and compliant research on UX Testbench.

1. Acceptable Research Use

UX Testbench is intended exclusively for ethical user research, usability testing, and product evaluation. The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Phishing & Credential Harvesting: Uploading prototypes or forms that simulate login portals or solicit passwords, private keys, or payment card credentials.
  • Unethical Data Collection: Soliciting sensitive personal data (e.g. government IDs, biometric data, health records) without explicit ethical board / IRB approval.
  • Testing on Minors: Conducting research with minors without documented parental or guardian consent.
  • Malware Distribution: Uploading prototype archives containing executable scripts, malware, or exploiting browser vulnerabilities.

2. Researcher Ethical Responsibilities

As a researcher running studies on UX Testbench, you agree to:

  • Clearly disclose the purpose of the study to participants before participation.
  • Provide an honest estimate of the time required.
  • Honor participants' rights to withdraw consent and request data deletion.

3. Moderation & Termination

The platform operators reserve the right to suspend accounts or take studies offline immediately if they violate these terms, generate abuse reports, or present a threat to participant safety.
