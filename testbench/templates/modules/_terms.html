{#- Plain words by default. When a technical term is the clearest word, wrap it in term(): a dotted underline, with the explanation on hover, on keyboard focus and on tap (a tap focuses the span), so it also works on touch screens, where a title tooltip never shows. -#} {% macro term(label, tip) -%}{{ label }}{%- endmacro %} {#- A translated sentence with a term inside it: pass t(key, term='@@TERM@@', ...) as text, since word order differs between languages. The sentence is escaped; only the term is markup. -#} {% macro tterm(text, label, tip) -%}{{ (text|e)|replace('@@TERM@@', term(label, tip)) }}{%- endmacro %}
